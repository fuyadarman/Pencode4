Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026 | Effective Immediately

Welcome to PenCode ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your development workflow remains private, secure, and under your full control. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, project files, and API communications.

Core Privacy Guarantee: PenCode operates primarily with local-first architecture. Your project source code, local files, and private keys remain on your device and are never sold, collected, or uploaded to our private servers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Because PenCode is an on-device autonomous AI coding environment, our data collection is minimal:

  • Local Project Files: All files, codebases, and databases created or imported into workspaces are stored locally in your device's app-sandboxed storage.
  • API Keys and Credentials: When you provide your own API keys (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or Groq), these credentials are saved locally in private app storage. They are only transmitted directly to the respective API endpoints specified by you.
  • Chat History & Agent Logs: Conversation logs, thoughts, and agent execution records are stored in a local SQLite/Room database on your device.

2. Third-Party AI Services & Cloud Connectors

When you initiate agent tasks or use external features, PenCode communicates directly with third-party providers upon your instruction:

  • AI Inference APIs: Prompts, context chunks, and code snippets are sent directly to the AI model provider you select (e.g., Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek). These communications are governed by the respective provider's terms and privacy policies.
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP) Servers: If you connect remote MCP servers, tool execution queries and arguments are routed strictly to the endpoints you designate.
  • Image & Media Generation: When requesting image or logo generation, prompts are submitted to visual generation providers (such as Pollinations AI) to create and return the image asset.

3. Permissions Used by PenCode

PenCode requests only standard Android permissions necessary for developer tooling:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to establish outbound HTTPS requests to AI inference providers, GitHub workflows, and remote MCP servers.
  • STORAGE / MEDIA (Photo Picker): Used exclusively when you explicitly attach an image or project asset into the workspace.
  • VIBRATE: Used for optional haptic feedback when AI tasks start or complete.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your project files, chats, and configurations are stored locally on your device:

  • You can clear workspace data, delete projects, or remove API keys at any time directly through the app settings or workspace screen.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored project files, databases, and saved keys.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement industry-standard practices to safeguard your data. Outbound network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Sensitive keys and tokens are confined within Android's private app sandbox isolation, inaccessible to other standard applications on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

PenCode is a developer tool and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
